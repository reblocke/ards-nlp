# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with
this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

For non-sensitive concerns, contact the maintainers through a repository issue. For confidential
reports, use [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/reblocke/ards-nlp/security/advisories/new)
and identify the submission as a Code of Conduct report. All reports will be reviewed promptly and
fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will use these Community Impact Guidelines:

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or
unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private warning explaining the violation and requesting a public apology when
appropriate.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior and a temporary restriction
from interaction with the people involved.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained
inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from interaction or public communication with the project.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violating community standards, harassment, or aggression
toward individuals or groups.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from public interaction within the project.
