# Data management

## Directory policy

- `data/raw/`:
  - immutable inputs
  - do not edit in-place
  - do not commit sensitive or large datasets

- `data/external/`:
  - downloaded artifacts (reference datasets, registries, etc.)
- standalone artifacts should have a provenance sidecar: `<filename>.source.json`; pinned external
  comparator clones and model files use tracked comparator config plus generated checksum audits

- `data/processed/`:
  - intermediate datasets generated by code

- `data/derived/`:
  - final analysis-ready datasets

## Provenance sidecars

For any downloaded file `data/external/foo.ext`, add `data/external/foo.ext.source.json` containing:

- source URL / DOI
- retrieval date
- version / tag / commit
- checksum (sha256)
- license
- any manual preprocessing notes

## Immutability

If you discover a problem in raw data:

- do not “fix” the raw file
- write a correction step in the pipeline and document it
- keep the corrected output in `data/processed/` or `data/derived/`

## MIMIC-CXR policy

- Do not commit MIMIC-CXR reports, extracted report text, images, MIMIC-CXR-JPG source files, RadGraph JSON, or report-level derived tables.
- Keep raw source files under `data/raw/` and locally generated Parquet files under `data/processed/`.
- Keep exported model-development artifacts and local modeling input caches under `data/derived/`.
- Keep completed text-stripped review exports under `data/derived/annotations/`.
- Keep QA summaries and manual-review CSV exports under `artifacts/qa/` and `artifacts/samples/`.
- Keep silver-label modeling metrics, plots, and fitted model artifacts under `artifacts/modeling/`.
- Do not commit prediction tables, fitted vectorizers, learned vocabularies, cached text extracts,
  completed review CSVs, or reviewer-level derived labels.

## CLAMP ARDS policy

- Treat CLAMP Stage 1 `.txt` inputs as raw selected report text.
- Keep real CLAMP workspace paths in ignored `config/config.yaml` or CLI flags.
- Keep CLAMP input files under `artifacts/restricted/clamp_ards/workspace/` by default, not in git.
- Keep CLAMP output files under `artifacts/restricted/clamp_ards/workspace/` by default, not in git.
- Keep returned combined and TXT-only output archives under `artifacts/restricted/clamp_ards/` and
  parse archive members directly rather than extracting large per-document trees.
- Keep the full TXT/XMI parity oracle under `artifacts/restricted/clamp_ards/oracle/`; stream XMI
  members directly and never unpack the archive.
- Keep CLAMP handoff manifests and summaries under `artifacts/restricted/clamp_ards/`.
- Treat the repo-local CLAMP workspace as staging only; synced descriptors target the configured
  CLAMP-machine `runtime_project_dir`.
- The CLAMP input manifest must not include raw report text.
- Keep parsed CLAMP entity and prediction tables under `data/derived/clamp_ards/`.
- Keep CLAMP-vs-silver aggregate diagnostics under `artifacts/clamp_ards/teacher_benchmark/`.
- Parsed entity rows may include evidence substrings in `entity_text`; do not commit them.
- Python-port entity rows also contain evidence substrings and remain ignored. Python prediction
  rows contain no report/entity text, but remain row-level restricted outputs.
- Restricted parity mismatch ledgers may contain MIMIC document IDs. They must replace entity text
  with hashes and remain under `artifacts/restricted/clamp_ards/python/`.
- Returned CLAMP tabular TXT files contain entity mentions even though they do not contain full
  reports; treat both the files and compact packet as restricted.
- Do not commit a copied live CLAMP workspace, CLAMP input packets, CLAMP output packets, or
  row-level restricted artifacts.
- Do not transfer full BigQuery/model-development extracts or raw MIMIC source files to the CLAMP
  machine. Transfer only the CLAMP project, generated `.txt` inputs, and needed manifests; return
  CLAMP outputs for parsing/merging on the MIMIC-enabled machine.

## REDCap annotation pilot policy

- Treat the Intermountain physician cohort as independent from the MIMIC comparator corpus.
- Keep real per-rater REDCap exports under ignored `data/restricted/` or another approved local
  restricted path.
- Keep `config/annotation_pilot.yaml` local and ignored because it contains real source paths.
- Drop `interpretation_text` during ingestion before constructing normalized analysis tables.
- Keep text-free rater-level and case-level outputs under `data/derived/annotations/pilot/`.
- Keep pilot QA, figures, disagreement review rows, and rendered reports under ignored
  `artifacts/annotations/pilot/` and `reports/annotation_pilot/`.
- Do not commit accession numbers, reviewer-level real data, disagreement review rows, or rendered
  real-data reports.
- Keep annotation planning outputs under ignored `artifacts/annotations/planning/` and
  `reports/annotation_planning/`; these outputs must remain aggregate and identifier-free.

## External comparator policy

- Keep external source clones, pretrained artifacts, NLTK data, transformer caches, and isolated
  virtual environments outside tracked package source.
- Keep the common MIMIC comparator packet under `artifacts/restricted/comparators/`; it contains
  report text and must never be committed or uploaded to an unapproved service.
- Keep canonical row-level predictions under `data/derived/comparators/` and aggregate diagnostics
  under `artifacts/comparators/`; all remain ignored.
- External processes receive report text but never silver labels. Prediction outputs contain
  identifiers, scores, labels, provenance, and hashes only.
- Verify pinned commits and model checksums before every pickle load. Pickles load only in their
  isolated subprocess/container.
- Treat UW missing weights and Afshar missing permission as explicit blocked statuses, not negative
  results or silently skipped models.
- Use the report-section fallback only when impression/findings is blank, and preserve its use as a
  text-free manifest provenance flag.
- Keep mismatched-target exploratory metrics separate from the primary bilateral-opacity metric
  tables.
- Keep Python CLAMP in the `compatibility_mirror` role and its aggregate metrics in separate
  compatibility outputs; it is software-parity evidence rather than an independent comparator.
